Who We Are

The RC3
Group.

RC3 Group is a Cincinnati-based multi-vertical holding company built on three principles: forensic transparency, corporate control, and elite standards.

Founded by a former Partner at an Am Law 100 firm with over 10 years of experience, every RC3 operation runs under one mandate — unapologetic excellence. Deep institutional knowledge of how systems actually operate, from the boardroom to the yard.
